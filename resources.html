Section VI  ·  For Administrators

For Department Heads & Administrators.

Economic Democracy is standards-completing, not standards-rejecting. The Standards Crosswalk maps all forty-five concepts and the teaching units against the Council for Economic Education's 2025 economics pillars and the C3 inquiry framework — full or near-full coverage across all eighteen pillars, including CEE's newest (Technology), with a single honest, supplementable light spot (formal GDP-accounting mechanics). The Educator Overview lays out the rationale, scope, and philosophy in four pages to share with your department before the first day.
